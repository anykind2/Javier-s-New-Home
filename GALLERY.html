A note on the work

Made badly, kept anyway.

Two things I tell myself whenever the sketchbook feels too unfinished to show anyone.

Why paper?

Paper doesn’t ask you to publish it.

A sketch can be bad in private. That is the whole point. Some of these drawings exist only because nobody was going to see them, and then the site happened. I’m still deciding whether that was a good idea.

Javier · pencil first, always
Why keep the film?

“It took one afternoon. It has outlived most of my serious plans.”

Dragon vs. Parrot is 21 seconds long and still makes me laugh. That is more than most things I’ve made on purpose.
