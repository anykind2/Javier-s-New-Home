    Liner notes

    How this archive works.

    Three things worth knowing before you hit shuffle and disappear for an afternoon.

    01 — The rules of the archive

    It is not ranked. It is remembered.

    • 01Every song here was chosen by a person, not a playlist.
    • 02Some titles and artists were lost to bad ID3 tags. I kept what I knew.
    • 03Favorites live in your browser. The music lives in the folder.
    • 04If a song is missing, it probably hasn’t been ripped yet.
    Javier · keeper of the folder
    02 — Why keep a local library?

    “The algorithm remembers what you like. A folder remembers who you were.”

    The same 64 files have survived four laptops, two phones, and every attempt to start over. They are the closest thing to a diary I have.

    On keeping things
    03 — A note on sound

    Headphones recommended.

    These are old MP3s with old artwork and old volume levels. Some are loud. Some are quiet. All of them are meant to be played loud enough to annoy the neighbours.

    No remastering planned
    04 — The player

    One tape, every page.

    Start a song here and it will keep playing while you wander into the notebook or the sketchbook. The dock at the bottom is always there if you need to pause.

    Space to pause · N for next
    05 — Credits

    These songs belong to their makers.

    Nothing here is sold, streamed for profit, or claimed as mine. It’s a personal shelf. If you love something, go find the artist and pay them properly.

    Support the people who made it
    The end, for now

    Stay a little longer.

    This place is a folder, a pair of headphones, and a pile of paper. It gets updated when something is worth keeping.
